What this page cannot know

The rules are hand-written, not fitted. Every threshold above was chosen on 2026-10-28 by reading the data, not by testing it. The methodology already admits the bear markets show no statistically significant lead over drawdowns; a trading rule built on them inherits that. ON means "the published rule is satisfied", not "this will work".

A token is not the share. xStocks and Ondo tokens track the price through an issuer that holds the shares; you hold a claim on the issuer. They trade when Nasdaq is shut, at whatever a thin book says, and the perps' oracles freeze at Friday's close. Basis between token, perp and share is a real cost on every hedge here, and it is widest exactly when you need the hedge.

Perps liquidate. Positions 2, 4, 5, 7 and 10 use leverage or are hedged with it. A weekend gap through a maintenance margin closes the position at the worst print of the week. The cards cap leverage for that reason; the venue does not.

Event markets resolve on their text. The bubble market needs three named triggers; the moratorium market needs a specific legal act; the recession contract follows a specific arbiter. A thesis being right is not the same as a market resolving YES.

Funding is unread. Position 4's whole return is a number this site does not fetch. The card states the hurdle and leaves the read to the venue; if that bothers you, the page is doing its job.

Eligibility is not universal – most spot and perp rails exclude US persons, Kalshi excludes everyone else, and the Treasury tokens split by investor class. Nothing here is an offer, a solicitation or advice. OMEN holds no positions and sells nothing.
